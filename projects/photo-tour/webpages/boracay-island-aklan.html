Boracay Island, Aklan

Boracay is a tropical paradise located in the Western Visayas region of the Philippines, renowned worldwide for its breathtaking beauty, vibrant culture, and world-class tourism. Despite its small size—measuring only about 7 kilometers long and roughly 1 kilometer wide at its narrowest point—it packs an incredible punch as one of the country's premier travel destinations.
