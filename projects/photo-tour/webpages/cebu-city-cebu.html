Cebu City, Cebu

Cebu, often referred to as the "Queen City of the South."

Basilica Minore del Santo Niño de Cebú, Cebu

The Basilica Minore del Santo Niño de Cebú, also known as the Santo Niño Church, is a 16th century minor basilica located in Cebu City, Philippines.

Magellan's Cross, Cebu

Magellan's Cross is one of the most historically significant landmarks in the Philippines. It symbolizes the introduction of Roman Catholicism and marks the birth of Christianity in the country.
