El Nido, Palawan

El Nido is a world-renowned coastal municipality located at the northern tip of Palawan island in the Philippines. Widely celebrated as one of the most breathtaking tropical destinations on Earth, it serves as the gateway to the Bacuit Archipelago—a stunning landscape of dramatic black-karst limestone cliffs, hidden lagoons, powdery white-sand beaches, and crystal-clear turquoise waters.
