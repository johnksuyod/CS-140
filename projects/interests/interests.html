The Best Friends: How I Met Your Mother

Ted "Evelyn" Mosby

"Kids, I'm gonna tell you an incredible story. The story of How I Met Your Mother."
Ted is a hopelessly romantic architect on a quest to find his perfect soulmate in New York City. The story begins with Ted Mosby telling his kids in 2030 how he finally met their mother. His tale is excruciatingly detailed, to the point that he recounts everything from his past relationships up to the day he met the Mother, Tracy McConnell.

Marshall Eriksen

"Lawyered!"

Marshall Eriksen, also known as Big Fudge and Vanilla Thunder, has been Ted Mosby's best friend since college and is the soulmate of Lily Aldrin. He is a law student who later became an environmental lawyer. He is very passionate about the environment.
