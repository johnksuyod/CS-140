Family Facts

  • They debuted as single-panel cartoons in The New Yorker starting in 1938.
  • The characters went without names for decades until the 1962 doll collection and the 1964 television series gave them identities
  • The last name comes from their creator, Charles Addams.
  • Creator Charles Addams originally wanted to name the patriarch "Repelly" (a play on repellent), but actor John Astin chose Gomez instead.
