John Cena is the greatest wrestler of all time! He is a 16-time WWE World Champion, and he has been the face of the franchise for over a decade. He lives by his principles of Hustle, Loyalty, and Respect. Aside from being a wrestler, he is also a Hollywood superstar.

Iconic Championship Matches

John Cena vs. CM Punk (Money in the Bank, July 17, 2011)
This is the greatest match of John Cena of all time! This is my favorite match even if John Cena lost to CM Punk.
John Cena vs. AJ Styles (Royal Rumble, January 29, 2017)
In this match, John Cena won using one of his signature moves twice! He used back-to-back Attitude Adjustments to secure the win against AJ Styles.
John Cena vs. Shawn Michaels (Raw, April 23, 2007)
This was a very long and thrilling match! It lasted for almost an hour, making it one of the longest matches in WWE history.
