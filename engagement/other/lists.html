How to Cook Adobo

Ordered list, the order of importance does matter.

  1. Marinite the chiken: Combine the chicken, soy sauce, and crushed garlic in a large bowl. Let it sit for at least 30 minutes to 1 hour.
  2. Sear the chicken: Heat the oil in a heavy pot over medium-high heat. Remove the chicken from the marinade and sear it for 2 to 3 minutes per side until nicely browned. Set the remaining marinade aside.
  3. Simmer: Pour the leftover marinade, water, whole black peppercorns, and bay leaves into the pot with the chicken. Bring to a boil, then lower he heat, cover, and simmer for 30 to 40 minutes until the chicken is tender.
  4. Add vinegar and redouce: Pour in the vinegar without stirring right away. Let it return to a simmer, then uncover the pot and cook for another 10 to 15 minutes until the sauce reduces to a rich, glossy glaze.
  5. Serve: Serve hot over a bed of steamed white rice.
