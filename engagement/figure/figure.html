What is the difference between block tags and inline tags?

Block tags start a new line and take up the full horizontal space. Inline tags are tags that usualy used inside block tags and follow the text. They only take necessary space that is needed. Not thw whole horizontal space.

What is the margin, border, and padding?

They are the layers of the box model. Margin is the outermost space of the block. Margin pushes neighboring tags. The Border is the edge of the box modle that wraps the padding and the content. The padding is the space inside the content from the boarder.

What are units of measurement you can use in CSS?

You can use the following units for measurements: cm (centimeters), mm (Millimeters), Q (Quarter-millimeters), in (Inches), pc (Picas), pt (Points), and px (Pixels).

When would you use a figure tag?

You use a figure tag when you want to add images that will be part of the main text that flows smoothly with the texts. It can be removed or removed without breaking the flow of the text
