What are the positives and negatives of hosting your videos on YouTube?

Hosting your videos on YouTube allows you to save some space, money and makes your webpages load faster. The downside is your video has ads./p>

What is the most useful thing you can do with a transform?

The most useful thing you can do with transform is rotate. You can use position:relative to translate instead of the transform:translate. You can always resize by resizing the actual file of the picture.

What is the difference between a transform and a transition?

Transform is for changing the shape, size, or position. While transition is for animations or effects in your webpage when clicking a link.
